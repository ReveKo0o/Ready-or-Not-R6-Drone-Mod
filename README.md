## Ready or Not R6 Drone Mod
<img width="1207" height="512" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c1b8a6cb-fa13-4009-88a5-2c429bd9a747" />


I have added the well-known drone from *Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege* to the game *Ready or Not*, you can use it to place pins, allowing you to mark and reveal the locations of enemies or innocent civillians to your teammates.
# Drone Features
## 1. The ability to jump
Just like in the game R6 (Not exactly the same since in the game R6, a drone can jump at an angle, however in this mod it only jumps straight up from where it’s positioned but it could still help you secure better positioning in certain scenarios.)
## 2. Ability to pin around (4-6 seconds)
As I mentioned in the mod's description, it includes a variety of helpful features such as the ability to mark nearby enemies, civillians for your teammates along with functions like watching their backs, clearing areas, gathering intel, spotting traps, and alerting others to the presence of traps, the condition of the room etc...
## 3. Not being detected by enemies
Enemies, suspects cant detect your drone, they do not act as if they have been seen. You can just pin them any time how you want.
# Usage
Download the **pakchunk99-Mods_R6Drone_P.pak** and **pakchunk99-Mod_SimpleBlueprintLoader.pak** (it works with BlueprintLoader) from source code or from the release section (.zip)
Open your game executable location example in steam, you can access it from the library
Select the Ready or Not from your library, right click and select the installed files and click the browse button

# Controls in game (Pay attention this part!)
!When you enter the game, wait about 2 seconds before pressing the drone key "7" this allows the server and the game itself to stabilize, helping you avoid potential issues later on.
## 1. To spawn your drone with key of "7" and when you press "7" again you can able to move your drone just like in siege
## 2. You can ping anywhere you want by pressing the "Z" key in your keyboard and if your crosshair looking to a has a collision object/asset/part (floor, walls, some roofs, enemies, civillians, doors, traps, windows etc.)
## 3. To jump just press space 
# Known issues (Might be fixed in the future or can add some settings to around it)
1. When you switch to the drone camera view, you may see your player model along with attachments, weapons, and items like helmets, vests, and grenades—floating in mid-air. (
However, this only appears that way from your own perspective while in drone view; your friends do not see your weapons floating around neither when they are in drone view themselves, nor when they are in standard FPS mode)
2. When you ping for a second it doesnt match the true distance but fixes quickly and when you press most of the time you will see for a 1 sec that "Text Box" text instead of meter i really dont know what they accusing this problem but i will try to fix

## 3. If every player in the lobby hops in the drone at the same time in the mission (not counting Los Suenos Police Headquarters/ The station) "Toc" always declares something like "mission failed" and cancels the operation, bringing it to an end; therefore, no matter what you do, stay in communication with your teammates, and ensure that not all players in the lobby switch to their drones at the same time
##  The most likely reason for this is that the game’s logic for switching to the drone which relies on the "possess" function—fails when there are no active players (i.e., when everyone is in drone mode); the system cannot locate or "see" any player, forcing it to prematurely end the mission.
## 4. This mod does not work in offline/singleplayer even with bots unf (at the moment)
